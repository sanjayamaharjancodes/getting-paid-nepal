Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information Getting Paid Nepal ("this site," "we," "us") collects from visitors and how it is used.

What we collect

This site is a static informational website. We do not currently operate a login system, and we do not directly ask you to submit personal information through a form on the site itself. If a Contact email is used, any information you send us (your email address, message content) is used only to respond to you and is not sold or shared with third parties.

Hosting and standard technical logs

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub Pages' infrastructure may log standard technical information (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) as part of ordinary web server operation. We do not directly control or access these logs beyond what GitHub's own platform provides; see GitHub's Privacy Statement for details on GitHub Pages hosting.

Affiliate links and third-party sites

This site has no active affiliate or referral links today (see our Affiliate Disclosure). When you click a link to a third-party site such as Payoneer, that company's own privacy policy governs any data you provide to them — this Privacy Policy does not extend to third-party sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any site you visit through a link on this page.

Cookies and analytics

We count page views and some link clicks with GoatCounter, which does not set cookies or build visitor profiles. The site's own code sets no cookies. Google's AdSense code can set cookies under this site's domain (such as __gads, in Google's list of advertising cookies) as well as on Google's own domains: see Advertising and cookies.

Advertising and cookies

Every page on Getting Paid Nepal loads Google's AdSense code, so Google may show ads here once it approves the site. Google chooses where those ads go and what they show, and they do not change what we write: we cover every payment route the same way.

How Google uses your data on this site: How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

Because this site loads the AdSense code, Google and other third parties may place and read cookies in your browser, and may use web beacons, IP addresses, local storage and similar identifiers, to collect information as a result of ad serving on this site.

Besides Google, other ad networks and ad technology companies may bid through Google to show ads here, and they may use their own cookies. For visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google publishes the list of ad technology partners it allows to serve and measure ads, each linked to a page explaining its data use. Google also publishes the ad vendors it has certified, with links to the vendors' websites: Google Ads Certified External Vendors and Ad Manager Certified External Vendors. You may visit a vendor's website to opt out of its use of cookies for personalized advertising, if the vendor offers this. You can also opt out of some of these companies' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info, linked below.

Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

Google requires sites that show its ads to ask visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland for consent before cookies or personal data are used for ads: see Google's consent management requirements for publishers. This site has no consent tool of its own. Any consent message here is Google's, shown through the same AdSense code, and Google records the choice you make in it.

Turning off personalized ads

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info/choices.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for freelancers, IT exporters, and general adult audiences, and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
